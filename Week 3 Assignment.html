These are some RPGs I've Enjoyed Recently

The Tomb Raider Trilogy

The Tomb Raider Trilogy is a cinematic masterclass when youre looking for an immersive world to explore through. You play as an archealogist, Lara Croft, who's name is known worldwide for discovering hidden truths in Ancient history that other archealogists had given up on.

Tomb Raider offers a wide range of beautifully designed terrain that is mostly explorable via rock climbing, shimmying, rappeling and scaling walls. A large p[ortion of these games feasture tombs often found in ruins, caves, cracks in the sides of a mountain or even underwater. These tombs always involve some sort of puzzle to reach the mural hidden deeper within. But Lara needs to find out these secrets before Trinity, a privately owned militia who have their sights set on the same discoveries with a lust for power, money and glory. Seeing as Lara always happesn to end up "in their way." Lara happens to be #1 on their shoot on sight list.

Cyberpunk 2077

Cyberpunk is an immersive first-person shooter game, that takes place in a future heavily influenced by technology and A.I. The world is in ruin and the population has been confined, to a few small cities, one of which is Night City. Night City is Las Vegas dessimated and rebuilt in the eyes of corporations and big-time city hustlers known as 'Fixers". You play as a self-employed mercenary, a common job in this lawless city, and make youre way to become a "Fixer" yourself.

Dying Light

Dying Light is a first-person shooter game that provides players with a unique experience revolving around parkour-style of movement while fighting to survive a zombie apocalypse. Players must find resources in the abandoned city to craft weapons and materials to survive.
