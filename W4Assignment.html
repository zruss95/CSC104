My Favorite Places to Vacation

Hawaii

I first visited Hawaii when I was 12 years old, and instantly it was one of my favorite places I'd ever been to. Hawaii has so many different terrains to explore throughout the island, all kinds of natural phenomena and more beautiful views than you could capture in a lifetime. The community thrives off tourism so there's always plenty of entertainment around and the culture is purely inviting. 10/10 would recommend.

Catalina Islands

Catalina Island is a beautiful island just off the coast of California that has traded hands between the U.S. and Mexico multiple times. The island itself is rather small but filled with nature, culture and history. Right when you get off the boat and have a look around the island gives off an old pirate rum-running vibe. The island had in fact been used for smuggling dating all the way back to the 1500s, pirates found that its small population, short distance to the mainland and the abundance of hidden coves made it a perfect hideout for them.

Puerto Penasco "Rocky Point"

Rocky Point is also known in Tucson as "Arizona's Beach" because its the closest beach we had access to. Puerto Penasco is a fantastic spot to get away though, the city thrives off tourism so there arre all kinds of activities to get involved in. There's scuba diving, parasailing, fishing, food and wine testing, museums, resorts, you name it. So if your looking for a good place nearby to get away on a budget, Puerto Penasco is the choice.
